Does your idea really need a feature?

A pause before building. Answer six questions and get a starting point to decide the next step.

  • 3 minutes
  • Free
  • No email required
1. Who needs this?

Can you name the person and the concrete situation?

2. What difficulty exists today?

Can you describe the problem without mentioning the solution?

3. What evidence do we have?

Is there evidence beyond an opinion?

4. What has already been tried?

Do you know the alternative used today and what already exists?

5. How will we know it helped?

Is there an observable sign of improvement defined?

6. What is the smallest useful test?

Do you know the smallest test and what could change the decision?
