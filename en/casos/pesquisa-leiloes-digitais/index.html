Platform Experience, digital auctions

Finding the right vehicle among thousands of options.

Digital used-vehicle auctions: how to connect search, information and buying experience across different markets.

The business context.

Auction buyers look for an opportunity that suits their business, not just a car. They need enough information about the condition and terms of each vehicle.

The challenge grows when supply spans markets with different attributes, data and presentation.

My role.

I work on the buyers' search journey, the redesign of the search interface and the evolution of the information APIs.

I coordinated releases in European markets with design and engineering, taking into account each market's data and operational differences.

The decisions behind the experience.

Start with the buyer's decision

Understand how they narrow down options and what information leads them to a vehicle's detail page, to tie filters to the real task.

Data as part of the experience

Filters only work with consistent attributes: shared terms, rules for incomplete data and a clear relationship between interface and APIs.

Consistency and local context

Standardise language, interaction patterns and the meaning of attributes, without erasing real differences between markets.

Delivery with learning built in

Functional validation, data quality and journey analysis, to find searches with no results and filters that stop helping.
