Social innovation, five days

AI for social good

Four prototypes and a research project. Brota was born from my contact with Plantar Lousada, MIGRO from the list I wish I'd had when I emigrated. The series turns everyday questions into experiences to explore.

One question a day.

Swipe or use the arrows to go through the five days.

Day 1

Brota

What makes someone come back to help?

Environmental volunteering that connects people with organisations caring for green spaces. Find actions near you, see the place and date and sign up. The Journey area brings your participations together and shows what was planted, with volunteer and organiser profiles.

Explore the prototype
Day 2

CEDA

How do you find a wheelchair for a few weeks?

Temporary loan of assistive equipment: browse, request, track the status and understand the return. A support team connects people and organisations and refers them for professional assessment when needed.

Explore the prototype
Day 3

Rumo

Where do you start when secondary school ends?

Free career guidance for young people: explore professions, skills and paths, compare routes and discover free courses. The My Rumo area gathers next steps into a plan.

Explore the prototype
Day 4

MIGRO

What do I need to do before, during and after the move?

For people emigrating: tasks by destination and stage, documents, deadlines and official sources, with progress tracking and a demo mode with a sample journey.

Explore the prototype
Day 5

Sponge City

Where does the rain go when a city is covered in concrete?

Research on urban rainwater: rain gardens, permeable paving and parks that retain water, with Porto's Parque Central da Asprela as an example, and sensors and digital models to understand where to intervene.
