Product Manager

I work in digital product in Brazil and Portugal: payments, super apps, marketplaces, retail media and internal platforms, at companies like Ame Digital, Magalu and Sonae. I've spent many hours in the meetings where people decide the colour of a button, the wording of a notification and the time it lands.

This book grew out of that experience. It isn't against technology: it's an invitation to understand the design choices competing for our attention and to use screens with more intention. I also talk about this on stage and on LinkedIn.
