Visit analytics

Only with your consent, Umami Cloud records pages visited, referrer, browser, device and clicks. The IP is used to estimate location. Data stays in the European Union for up to six months and is never sold or used for advertising.

Browser preferences and security

The site stores your language and analytics choice for 180 days. Cloudflare uses the security cookie __cf_bm to detect automated traffic; it expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Security and support services may operate outside the EU.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, where applicable. You can withdraw analytics consent under "Analytics preferences" in the footer, and lodge a complaint with the CNPD.
